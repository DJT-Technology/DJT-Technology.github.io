Capabilities

Capabilities organised around the manufacturing process.

Four connected areas: new product introduction, material control, inspection and testing, and production data with traceability.

Explore Our Capabilities
  1. 01

    New Product Introduction & Manufacturing Readiness

    DJT begins new product introduction by reviewing customer-provided documentation, manufacturing requirements and process risks. We address manufacturability (DFM), assembly processes, inspection requirements and build readiness to identify potential issues before production.

  2. 02

    Material Management & Digital Control

    Accurate material control is essential to reliable PCB assembly. DJT aligns material identification, BOM revisions, storage requirements and production orders, with lot-control and loading-verification requirements reviewed for each project.

  3. 03

    Inspection, Testing & Programming

    Inspection and testing are considered throughout manufacturing, not only at final release. DJT combines process inspection methods such as SPI and AOI with project-specific ICT, functional testing and device programming where applicable. For dedicated test solutions, our engineering team coordinates fixture and program introduction, setup and verification with customers and specialist partners.

  4. 04

    Production Data & Traceability

    DJT uses MES and ERP systems to support production management and manufacturing records. Process information is recorded according to actual manufacturing operations and project requirements, providing a basis for production reviews, issue investigation and quality traceability. Traceability coverage reflects the information captured and retained for each project.
