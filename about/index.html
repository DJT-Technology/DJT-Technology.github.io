About DJT

A Shanghai manufacturing partner for electronics programs.

Founded in 2001, DJT (大佳田) has focused on SMT and PCB assembly for 26 years. We operate two parallel factories in Shanghai — Jiading, which handles more high-end manufacturing and is usually preferred for visits, and Pudong. We are a pure-play assembly partner: process craftsmanship and reliable delivery, not own-brand products.
